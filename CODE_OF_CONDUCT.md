# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Review ideas and behavior, not a
person's identity or background. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
unwanted private contact, and publishing personal information are not
acceptable.

Keep issues and reviews relevant to the project. Explain disagreements with
evidence; acknowledge mistakes and respect maintainer decisions on scope/safety.
Do not post secrets, private desktop contents, or security exploit details.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate contributions or restrict
participation. Raise conduct concerns privately through a verified maintainer
contact. The final repository's confidential reporting channel must be
established before inviting wider community participation; do not assume public
issues are private.
